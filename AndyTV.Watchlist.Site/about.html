About AndyTV Watchlist

AndyTV Watchlist helps sports fans decide what to watch. It brings notable games and events across sports into one daily guide, with ranked recommendations, start times, TV or streaming information when available, and a schedule view.

How the watchlist is made

Event feeds, including public ESPN scoreboards and curated sports schedules, provide candidate games and event details. An automated research process ranks selected events and adds context using web sources; links to source articles are included with recommendations when available. Event schedules and listed networks are subject to change, so check the broadcaster for the latest information.

Who makes it

AndyTV Watchlist is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the leagues, teams, broadcasters, or publications mentioned on the site.
