Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

AndyTV Watchlist (andytv.today) respects your privacy. We don't have accounts, and we don't ask for your name or email.

Information We Collect

Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. We use this to operate and improve the site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are viewed and approximately where visitors are from. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes data like your IP address, device, and browser information. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Local Storage

Your browser stores preferences, such as your light/dark theme choice, on your device. This information is not sent to us.

Third-Party Services

The site is hosted on Microsoft Azure and loads scripts from third-party providers (such as Google and jsDelivr). These providers may receive your IP address and browser information when you visit. Links to other websites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us on X at @andytvwatchlist. Learn more on our About page.

We may update this policy as needed.
